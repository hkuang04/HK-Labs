HK Labs ("we") builds booking automation for small businesses. This page explains what information our app accesses and how it is used. We operate from Canada.

What the app accesses

When a business owner connects their Google account, our app can access only the parts they approve:

  • Google Calendar, to add and check booking appointments.
  • Google Sheets, to save booking details and a contact list.
  • Gmail, to send booking confirmation emails from the owner's account.

What information is handled

When a customer submits a booking form, their name, email, phone number, address, requested service and preferred date and time are processed so the booking can be recorded and confirmed.

How it is used

Information is used only to run that business's booking system: creating the calendar event, saving the booking, and sending the owner and customer their messages. We do not sell the information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else. Booking details are not kept by us after the automation runs; they remain in the business owner's own Google account.

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Notifications

Booking alerts to the business owner may be sent through Telegram or email.

Removing access or data

A business owner can remove our app's access at any time from the permissions page of their Google account. To ask us to delete anything, or with any privacy question, use the contact button below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.

Contact

Questions about your privacy, or a request to delete your information? Send us a message.
