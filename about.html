We build simple booking systems for small businesses.

QuexFlow sets up online booking that runs on the tools you already use, so customers can book you and you can get on with the work.

What we do

We are an independent automation studio in Canada. We start with online booking for small businesses, and build other automations, including AI assistants, when a business needs them.

A customer scans a QR code or opens a link and picks a time. The booking appears on your calendar, you get an alert on your phone through Telegram, a free app, and your customer gets a confirmation email. If a time is already taken, we tell you and ask the customer to choose another.

Your customers are saved to a contact list in your own Google workspace, so you never have to type them in.

What we believe

  • Your data stays yours

    Everything runs inside your own Google workspace. Your bookings, contacts and calendar belong to you, and you can remove our access whenever you like.

  • Simple beats clever

    A booking system should be something you forget about. We keep the setup small and the messages clear.

  • Built around how you work

    There is no new software to learn. If you already use Google Calendar and a phone, you already know how to use it.

Working with us

  1. We learn how customers book you today

    A short conversation about your services, your hours and how bookings reach you now.

  2. We build it and connect your accounts

    You sign in to your own Google account yourself. We never need your password.

  3. We test it with you

    We try real bookings together, then hand over your QR code and link.

  4. We keep it running

    If something breaks, we hear about it first. If you want a change, we make it.

Where we work

We are based in Canada and work with small businesses here. Setup is done online, and we handle the technical parts so you do not have to.
