HK Labs

Booking automation for small businesses in Canada.

HK Labs builds simple online booking systems for small businesses and tradespeople. A customer scans a QR code or opens a link and fills out a short form. The job goes into the owner's calendar and contact list, the owner is alerted right away, and the customer gets an automatic confirmation.

Each system runs on the business owner's own Google account (Calendar, Sheets and Gmail), so the owner keeps their own data.
