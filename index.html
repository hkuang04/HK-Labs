How a booking works

  1. The customer scans

    They scan your QR code or open your link and fill in a short form: name, phone, email, address, the service they need and a preferred time.

  2. The job is saved

    It goes on your Google Calendar with the customer's details, and the customer is added to your contact list in Google Sheets.

  3. You get an alert

    A message arrives on Telegram with everything you need. If that time is already taken, it tells you instead of double-booking.

  4. The customer hears back

    They receive a confirmation email, or a note asking them to choose another time if the slot was taken.

Made for people who are on the job, not at a desk

  • A QR code you can put anywhere On the truck, business cards, invoices and flyers. It always opens your booking form.
  • Bookings in the calendar you already use Each job appears on your Google Calendar with the address and the customer's details.
  • A contact list that builds itself Every customer who books is saved to a Google Sheet that belongs to you.
  • Alerts that reach you on site A message on your phone the moment a booking comes in, so you are not checking another inbox.
  • Protection against double-booking If a time is already taken, you are warned, and the customer is asked to pick another.

Your Google account, Your data

Each booking system runs on the business owner's own Google account. HK Labs connects to Google Calendar, Google Sheets and Gmail only to record bookings and send confirmation emails.

Bookings and customer details stay in your Google account. You can remove access at any time from your Google account's permissions page.
