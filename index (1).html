How a booking works

  1. The customer scans

    They scan your QR code or open your link and fill in a short form: name, phone, email, address, the service they need and a preferred time.

  2. The job is saved

    It goes on your Google Calendar with the customer's details, and the customer is added to your contact list in Google Sheets.

  3. You get an alert

    A message arrives on Telegram with everything you need. If that time is already taken, it tells you instead of double-booking.

  4. The customer hears back

    They receive a confirmation email, or a note asking them to choose another time if the slot was taken.

Common questions

What do my customers see?

A short form that opens from your QR code or link. They choose a date and time, and receive a confirmation email afterwards.

What if a time is already booked?

You get a message saying it was not added to your calendar, and the customer receives an email asking them to pick another time.

How much does it cost?

A one-time setup fee and a small monthly fee. We quote after a short chat, because every business books a little differently.

What do I need to get started?

A Google account for your calendar and contacts, and a phone for booking alerts. We handle the rest.

Do I need to be technical?

No. We set it up and connect your accounts. After that, bookings show up on your calendar and on your phone.

Who owns the booking information?

You do. Bookings and contacts are stored in your own Google Calendar and Google Sheets.

Can I stop whenever I want?

Yes. You can remove HK Labs' access from your Google account at any time, and your calendar and sheets stay with you.
